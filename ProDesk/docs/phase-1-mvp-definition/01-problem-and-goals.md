01 - Problem and Goals
1. Project Overview

ProDesk is an online marketplace designed to connect music producers (Producers) with artists (Artists) through a centralized platform for discovering, purchasing, and selling music beats.

The platform provides an environment where Producers can showcase their musical creations, manage their published beats, reach potential customers, and generate revenue from their work. At the same time, Artists can explore a diverse collection of beats, find music that matches their artistic vision, and purchase licenses that allow them to use those beats in their musical projects.

In addition to standard beat sales, ProDesk aims to provide a dedicated environment for custom beat orders, allowing Artists to request personalized music productions from Producers.

2. Problem Statement

The process of discovering, purchasing, and selling music beats is often scattered across different platforms, social networks, and communication channels.

Artists may face several challenges when looking for suitable beats, including:

Difficulty discovering high-quality beats that match their musical style and creative requirements.
Lack of structured information about beats, such as genre, subgenre, mood, BPM, and musical key.
Uncertainty about usage rights, licensing conditions, and commercial permissions.
Difficulty comparing different Producers and evaluating their work.
Lack of a centralized and organized purchasing experience.
Challenges in communicating with Producers and requesting custom-made beats.

On the other hand, Producers may encounter difficulties such as:

Limited opportunities to showcase and monetize their musical creations.
Difficulty managing beat listings, versions, prices, and sales.
Lack of centralized tools for tracking sales and revenue.
Difficulty building a professional profile and establishing credibility.
Challenges in managing custom production requests and communicating with potential customers.

These problems create a need for a specialized marketplace that brings Artists and Producers together in a structured, transparent, and user-friendly environment.

ProDesk aims to address these challenges by providing a centralized platform that simplifies the entire process of discovering, publishing, purchasing, and managing music beats.

3. Project Goals

The primary goal of ProDesk is to create a reliable and accessible marketplace that facilitates commercial interactions between music Producers and Artists.

The key objectives of the platform include:

3.1. For Artists
Provide access to a diverse catalog of music beats.
Enable advanced beat discovery and filtering based on musical characteristics.
Allow Artists to preview beats before purchasing.
Provide clear licensing information and usage permissions.
Simplify the purchasing process.
Allow Artists to manage their purchases and access their purchased files.
Provide the ability to request custom-made beats.
Enable Artists to review and rate Producers based on their experiences.
3.2. For Producers
Provide professional profiles for showcasing musical work.
Enable publishing, editing, and managing beats.
Support multiple beat versions and licensing options.
Provide tools for managing prices and product information.
Offer sales and revenue statistics.
Enable Producers to receive and manage custom beat requests.
Help Producers build credibility through customer ratings and reviews.
Provide insights into beat views and preview plays.
3.3. Platform-Wide Goals
Establish a secure and organized purchasing environment.
Ensure transparency in licensing and usage permissions.
Provide role-based access for Artists, Producers, and Administrators.
Maintain reliable management of users, purchases, and content.
Build a scalable architecture that supports future platform growth.
Provide a consistent and user-friendly experience across the platform.
4. Target Audience

ProDesk primarily targets individuals and professionals working in the music production industry.

4.1. Artists

Artists are users who need instrumental beats for creating songs, recordings, and other musical projects.

They use ProDesk to discover suitable beats, preview available tracks, purchase licenses, and request custom productions.

4.2. Music Producers

Producers are users who create instrumental music and want to distribute and sell their work.

They use ProDesk to publish beats, manage their products, receive orders, track sales, and develop their professional presence.

4.3. Administrators

Administrators are responsible for managing and maintaining the platform.

Their responsibilities include:

Managing user accounts and roles.
Monitoring published beats and platform content.
Managing platform activities and transactions.
Handling reports and disputes.
Maintaining platform integrity and security.
5. Value Proposition

ProDesk creates value by bringing Artists and Producers together in one specialized marketplace.

Value for Artists
Access to a centralized collection of beats.
Easier discovery through structured musical metadata.
Ability to preview beats before purchasing.
Transparent licensing and purchasing conditions.
Access to professional Producers.
Opportunity to request personalized music productions.
Value for Producers
A dedicated marketplace for selling musical creations.
Greater visibility and access to potential customers.
Centralized beat and order management.
Sales and performance statistics.
Professional profiles with ratings and reviews.
Opportunities to generate revenue through beat sales and custom orders.
Value for the Platform
Creation of a specialized ecosystem for beat trading.
Facilitation of commercial relationships between Artists and Producers.
Establishment of a structured environment for music licensing.
Potential for future expansion into additional music-related services.
6. Project Vision

The long-term vision of ProDesk is to become a comprehensive marketplace for music production resources and services.

Beyond standard beat sales, the platform aims to evolve into an ecosystem where Artists and Producers can discover each other, collaborate, exchange creative services, and establish long-term professional relationships.

By combining beat discovery, licensing, purchasing, custom production requests, and professional profiles, ProDesk seeks to make the process of creating and commercializing music more accessible, organized, and efficient.
